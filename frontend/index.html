Principle

Careers deserve engineering rigor.

Traditional career advice relies on arbitrary checklists and recruiter intuition. Candidates are told they "need more experience" without receiving a quantitative breakdown of the exact conceptual missing components.

SkillGraph models career qualification as a directed graph. Roles are curated ontologies with explicit prerequisite chains, core competencies, and required hours. Your resume is parsed deterministically with zero buzzword inflation.

Capabilities

Every layer of the career graph, quantified.

01

Resume Ingestion

Extract plain text and structured skills from PDF and DOCX documents in memory. Zero binary byte retention.

02

Skill-Gap Analysis

Compare candidate profiles against 11 curated role ontologies. Delineate core, recommended, and optional gaps.

03

Job Description Match

Paste unstructured job descriptions to segment required vs. preferred criteria and detect tenure expectations.

04

6-Week Roadmap

Dynamic weekly progression ordering foundational concepts before advanced tiers with weekly study caps.

05

Interactive Checklist

Toggle completion status with optimistic updates and SQLite persistence surviving application restarts.

06

Security & IDOR Isolation

PBKDF2 salted passwords, JWT bearer tokens, and strict ownership validation on all user resources.
