Principle

Careers deserve engineering rigor.

Traditional career advice relies on arbitrary checklists and recruiter intuition. Candidates are told they "need more experience" without receiving a quantitative breakdown of the exact conceptual missing components.

SkillGraph models career qualification as a directed graph. Roles are curated ontologies with explicit prerequisite chains, core competencies, and required hours. Your resume is parsed deterministically with zero buzzword inflation.

Workflow

How SkillGraph works.

From your current background to your target engineering role in seven clear, evidence-based steps.

01

Create your profile

Specify your career stage, current technical background, and initial target role so benchmarks reflect your trajectory.

02

Upload your resume

Ingest your PDF or DOCX resume. SkillGraph extracts technical skills, tools, and experience deterministically in memory.

03

Review your extracted skills

Inspect detected proficiencies, remove false positives, and add unlisted competencies to establish an accurate baseline.

04

Explore and compare career roles

Benchmark your skillset against 11 curated role ontologies and compare overlapping competencies across adjacent career paths.

05

Discover skill gaps and career readiness

Review your quantitative readiness score, matched core skills, and exact missing prerequisites on your personal dashboard.

06

Connect your coding profile

Link your public GitHub activity and LeetCode handle to prove real shipping evidence, languages used, and problem-solving readiness.

07

Follow your personalized roadmap

Execute a tailored 6-week curriculum with weekly milestones, capstone projects, and persistent task checkboxes.
